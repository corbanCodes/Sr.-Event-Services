How it works

Keep it simple

  1. 1. Tell us about your event — date, venue, headcount, and what the night needs.
  2. 2. Pick your services — staffing, china & tableware, or both (or let us recommend).
  3. 3. Adding the bar? We loop in TipsyT and the booking runs through her site — one plan, two teams.
  4. 4. Relax & enjoy while we set, serve and reset. Six-hour minimum; travel fee outside our local area, always quoted up front.
